## I. Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) outlines the rules and guidelines for the appropriate and responsible use of the **BoardQuest AI-Gamified MTLE Review Platform**. The purpose of this policy is to define the acceptable standards for accessing the platform, ensuring system integrity, data protection, and equity across all reviewees. By establishing clear operational rules and technical boundaries, this policy protects proprietary AI-synthesized review materials, safeguards user privacy, and ensures a fair, reliable learning environment for candidates preparing for the Medical Technologist Licensure Examination (MTLE) at SWU PHINMA.

---

## II. Scope
This policy applies to all authorized users interacting with the BoardQuest platform across all operational environments (**Production, User Acceptance Testing, and Testing**). Access to BoardQuest is a privilege granted in connection with one's academic or administrative role.

Covered roles explicitly include:
- **Students / Aspirants:** Medical Technology reviewees and graduating interns.
- **Faculty & Coordinators:** Subject matter instructors, activity admins, and review coordinators.
- **Administrators & Technical Staff:** Faculty Leads, Super Admins, Development Team members, and Quality Assurance reviewers.
- **External Partners:** Authorized review center representatives.

---

## III. Allowed Uses
Users are expected to act as responsible stewards of exam content and student data. You are authorized to utilize the BoardQuest platform strictly for legitimate academic review and instructional management purposes:

1. **Personal Exam Preparation (Aspirants):** Accessing study modules, daily missions, adaptive practice quizzes, and battle simulation modules within the Quest Arena (including **Learning Mode** with instant AI feedback and **Test Mode** under timed constraints) for personal licensure preparation.
2. **Analytics & Performance Tracking (Aspirants):** Reviewing individual diagnostic domain radar charts, tracking simulated Board Readiness Metrics, and viewing peer leaderboards to maintain study motivation and address learning gaps.
3. **Faculty Content Management (Faculty & Coordinators):** Uploading, editing, and organizing validated board-style review items and AI-synthesized practice question banks across the six major laboratory science domains (*Clinical Chemistry, Microbiology, Hematology, Clinical Microscopy, Immunohematology, Histopathology & Laws*).
4. **Institutional Oversight (Administrators):** Reviewing aggregated, anonymized cohort performance analytics and exporting quantitative reports to guide targeted instructional strategies.

---

## IV. Prohibited Uses
The following activities are strictly prohibited and constitute a direct violation of this AUP:

- **Workflow and Data Integrity Violations:**
  - Using automated scripts, bots, web crawlers, or external computational tools to alter quiz responses, manipulate daily streaks, or artificially inflate XP/scores on public leaderboards.
  - Attempting to exploit software bugs or circumvent authentication protocols to gain unauthorized administrative access.
- **Confidentiality and Data Privacy Violations:**
  - Scraping, extracting, reverse-engineering, downloading, screenshotting, or distributing BoardQuest proprietary question banks, AI-generated test items, or source code to unauthorized third parties or public repositories.
- **System Misuse:**
  - Sharing personal login credentials, session tokens, or account access with any individual or entity under any circumstances.
  - Failing to comply with institutional password standards requiring a **minimum of 10 characters with mixed types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols)**.
  - Installing unauthorized software, browser extensions, or unlicensed tools on platform-connected devices.
  - Misusing institutional email or internet connections for non-academic activities.
  - Uploading, storing, or transmitting blocked and prohibited content (including uncertified PRC test items, malicious scripts, or non-curricular media).

---

## V. Disciplinary Action Matrix & Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to adhere to this policy compromises system security and academic equity. Violations will result in disciplinary action based on severity and frequency:

| Violation Severity | Possible Disciplinary Action | Responsible Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minor** | Verbal warning, forced session logout, and required password reset. | System Administrator |
| **Moderate** | Formal written warning, temporary suspension of BoardQuest access, and notification of the respective department head. | Department Head |
| **Severe** | Immediate and permanent revocation of BoardQuest platform account access, invalidation of mock exam analytics, zero-grading of platform-derived performance scores, and referral to institutional disciplinary or human resources boards. | University Disciplinary Board |

---

## VI. Category of Offenses
- **Minor Offenses:** Unintentional procedural lapses regarding device security (e.g., Session Negligence, leaving an active device unattended in a supervised lab).
- **Moderate Offenses:** Unauthorized actions that disrupt device security protocols but do not intentionally breach exam integrity (e.g., Accessing via Compromised Hardware, allowing Unauthorized Access).
- **Severe Offenses:** Critical violations that directly compromise academic integrity or data privacy (e.g., Data Compromise, System Manipulation of gamified scores, script cheating).
