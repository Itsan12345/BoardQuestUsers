## I. Policy Statement & Purpose
This Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy outlines the acceptable use, requirements, and responsibilities for users who access the **BoardQuest AI-Gamified MTLE Review Platform** using personally owned devices (laptops, tablets, smartphones).

The purpose of this policy is to:
- Enable flexible, secure access to BoardQuest for adaptive gamified learning quests, question bank uploads, diagnostic assessments, and performance analytics without requiring institution-issued hardware.
- Protect the confidentiality of proprietary AI-synthesized exam content and sensitive student performance data accessed through personal devices.
- Establish clear expectations for the secure and appropriate use of personal devices when connecting to the platform.

---

## II. Scope & Compliance
This policy applies to all authorized personnel utilizing personal devices to access BoardQuest, explicitly including:
- **Students / MTLE Reviewees (Aspirants)**
- **Faculty Admins & Teachers**
- **Subject Team Leads & Deans**
- **System Developers & Super Admins**
- **External Partner Review Center Staff**

Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in the immediate revocation of BoardQuest access privileges, alongside further institutional disciplinary measures.

---

## III. Definition of Key Terms
- **Acceptable Use:** Using a personal device responsibly and securely to perform legitimate, role-appropriate BoardQuest tasks (e.g., completing Quest Arena battles, uploading questions, reviewing cohort analytics).
- **Bring Your Own Device (BYOD):** Allowing BoardQuest users to access the platform via personally owned technology devices.
- **Cookies / Session Tokens:** Small temporary files stored on a user's device by a web browser, explicitly required by BoardQuest to maintain secure, active login sessions.
- **Corporate / Academic Data:** Confidential institutional assets and intellectual property owned by Southwestern University PHINMA and the BoardQuest project team, encompassing AI-generated MTLE exam logic questions, answer keys, diagnostic assessment frameworks, syllabi, CMS authoring modules, and Quest Arena battle mechanics.
- **MTLE:** The official Medical Technologist Licensure Examination in the Philippines.
- **Personal Device:** Any personally owned electronic device capable of connecting to the internet and accessing BoardQuest.
- **Personal Data:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and confidential user records stored or processed within BoardQuest, including student test scores, diagnostic radar charts, cohort analytics, account credentials, and active session tokens.
- **Public Data:** Non-sensitive information approved for unrestricted public disclosure (e.g., technical system requirements, public review announcements).
- **Session Hygiene:** The mandatory security practice of actively logging out of the BoardQuest platform and locking a device whenever it is left unattended.
- **Unacceptable Use:** Any activity that violates this policy, the BoardQuest AUP, or local/national laws (e.g., leaking exam content, automated script cheating, credential sharing).
- **VPN (Virtual Private Network):** A secure, encrypted network connection recommended to protect institutional data when accessing BoardQuest over unsecured public Wi-Fi.

## IV. Device Registration Process
Prior to accessing proprietary BoardQuest modules, all personal devices must complete formal registration:
1. **Prerequisite Check:** User verifies that their personal device runs a supported OS and browser with security patches updated within the last 30 days.
2. **Registration Submission:** User submits an electronic *Device Registration Request* to the System Administrator containing their institutional ID, device model, and primary operating system details.
3. **Verification & Provisioning:** The System Administrator verifies user identity, validates device parameters, and authorizes the device profile within the platform.
4. **Session Token Issuance:** Upon first authentication, the system binds an encrypted local session token to the registered device to allow secure access.

## V. User Responsibilities & Security Requirements

### 5.1. Device Care and Security
- **Physical Security:** Users are solely responsible for protecting their personal device from loss, theft, damage, or misuse.
- **Device Lock:** Devices must be secured with a strong password, PIN, or biometric lock (fingerprint/Face ID) that is never shared with others.
- **Session Hygiene:** Users must log out of BoardQuest and lock their device whenever unattended.
- **Software Updates:** Personal devices must have all OS and web browser security patches applied within **30 calendar days** of vendor release. Devices running unsupported or end-of-life operating systems (e.g., Windows 7, iOS 13 or older) are strictly prohibited.

### 5.2. Acceptable Use
- **Role-Appropriate Access:** Personal devices may only be used to perform tasks appropriate to the user's assigned role.
- **Confidentiality:** Users must NOT screenshot, download, print, or export AI-generated logic questions, answer keys, or diagnostic radar charts for unauthorized distribution.
- **Secure Network Use:** Accessing BoardQuest over unsecured public Wi-Fi networks is strictly prohibited unless connected through a trusted VPN. All transmissions must occur over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS).
- **No Bypassing Security:** Attempting to bypass login security, session controls, or role-based access restrictions from a personal device is strictly prohibited.

### 5.3. Unacceptable Use
- **Data Compromise:** Leaking, sharing, or distributing confidential exam content or student performance data.
- **Session Negligence:** Leaving active sessions logged in and unattended.
- **System Manipulation:** Installing browser extensions, bots, or scripts to scrape data, alter response timers, or artificially inflate Experience Points (XP).
- **Compromised Hardware:** Accessing the platform from a jailbroken, rooted, or maliciously modified device.
- **Unauthorized Access:** Permitting unauthorized individuals to view or operate your active account.

University IT and the BoardQuest DevTeam strictly assist with **platform-specific issues** (e.g., account login failures, server errors, scorecard dispatch). They are **not responsible** for troubleshooting, repairing, or maintaining personal devices, local third-party software conflicts, or physical hardware failures.

## VII. Institutional Rights, Disclaimers & Protocols

### 7.1. Monitoring and Access Review
Southwestern University PHINMA reserves the right to monitor and log all BoardQuest account activity performed from personal devices. Users have no expectation of privacy regarding their platform activity.

### 7.2. Liability Disclaimer
Southwestern University PHINMA and the BoardQuest project team assume no responsibility or financial liability for personal device damage, loss, theft, data usage costs over cellular networks, or device hardware/software issues. Users operate personal devices at their own risk.

### 7.3. Lost or Stolen Device Protocol
If a personal device used to access BoardQuest is lost, stolen, or compromised:
1. **Reporting (within 24 Hours):** Report the incident immediately to the BoardQuest System Administrator / IT Helpdesk.
2. **Immediate Containment:** The System Administrator will immediately terminate all active local session tokens, force log out all active sessions, and temporarily suspend account access.
3. **Account Restoration:** Account access remains suspended until password resets are completed and the user registers a new, validated secure device.

### 7.4. Offboarding Procedures
- **Student Offboarding:** Upon MTLE cycle completion, graduation, or withdrawal, access is automatically revoked. All active session tokens on personal devices are forcefully invalidated.
- **Faculty & Personnel Offboarding:** Upon resignation, termination, or contract completion, user accounts are deactivated on or before the final working day. The System Administrator executes an immediate remote session revocation to purge cached permissions and cut off access to CMS authoring modules and cohort analytics.
- **Device Change / Replacement:** When a user upgrades or sells a registered personal device:
  1. Log out of all active BoardQuest sessions and clear local browser data/cookies.
  2. Submit a device unregistration request to the System Administrator.
  3. Re-register the new device via the standard BYOD Device Registration Process.
